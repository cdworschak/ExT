04 — Exploration

Exploration

What happens between fights — reading a room, and covering ground.

Reaction Check

Roll 2d10 + CHA vs target 12, shiftable by the scene.

TotalDisposition
≤9Hostile
10–11Wary
12–14Apathetic
15–16Friendly
≥17Helpful

Travel

GM rolls a d6 for days until a Travel Event (d8 with a relevant wilderness talent or toolkit; −1 on treacherous terrain).

d6Event
1Mishap — add 1 day
2–3Random Encounter
4–5Failed to Gather Food — mark a ration or become Deprived
6Good Fortune — subtract 1 day
TransportAdjustment
On footNo change
Mount/cartHalf time; animals need rations on a 4–5
Riverboat/glider/airshipTwo-thirds reduction
Forced marchOne-third reduction; daily ration or Deprived

Exploration Roll

While exploring, GM rolls a d6 every Turn (~10 min).

d6Event
1Encounter
2Encounter Sign — a 1–2 on the next roll becomes that encounter
3Light — mark a usage dot
4–6Nothing
