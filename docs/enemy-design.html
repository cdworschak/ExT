Enemy Design

How to build anything the party fights, from a single number instead of a full stat block.

Enemy Design Philosophy

Every check defaults to target 12 — the same target PCs use.

Enemy Generation Procedure

  1. Mook or Named? Mook: no Wound slot, Guard alone. Named: full Guard → Wound → Scar chain.
  2. Pick one Base Target (Weak/Standard/Strong, or anywhere between).
  3. Pick baseline Guard: Mook 4–6, Named mid-tier 8–12, Named boss 12–20+.
  4. Pick a weapon tier — Light/Medium/Heavy/Massive.
  5. Pick a flat damage bonus — Mooks +0 to +1, mid-tier named +2 to +3, bosses +4.
  6. If Named, pick Wound slots (1–3).
  7. Note exceptions — shift one Target use with a one-line reason. Shock (a monster-specific trait) is a fifth exception category.
  8. Apply Encounter Level Scaling for the target party level.
Worked Example — Building a Monster

A level-6 party (3 milestones). The GM wants a Droaam Ogre-Kin Enforcer — a named lieutenant. Base Target: Strong (15), with an attack exception at Target 12 — tough to hit, but not as sharp on offense. Baseline Guard: 14. Weapon: Heavy (1d8), damage bonus +3. Wound slots: 2.

Scaling for 3 milestones: Guard 14+3=17. Damage +3+1=+4. Base Target 15+1=16 (the attack exception scales too: 12+1=13). Wound slots stay at 2, since the third-slot threshold isn't reached until milestone 3+ specifically for Multi-Action bosses.

Final stat block: Guard 17, 2 Wound slots, Target 16 to hit or resist, Target 13 to attack, deals 1d8+4. Seven numbers, decided in seven quick choices — no separate stat block written out by hand.

Legendary Resistance

A true solo boss auto-succeeds its first 3 failed resistance checks per encounter. Without this, sustained control from 2–3 dedicated casters can lock a boss out of acting almost entirely.

A boss's resist Target is just its Base Target, scaled like everything else — no separate stat needed, unless a specific monster has a resist exception (a Lich's undead mind, a Dragon's magic-soaked hide). Applies only to named threats built with Multi-Action.
