10 — Advancement

Advancement

How the party grows — XP, leveling, and the Vertical/Lateral tracks.

Experience & Leveling

Level up at XP = 5× current level, resetting to 0 after each level-up — level 2 needs a fresh 5 XP, level 3 a fresh 10, level 4 a fresh 15, and so on up through level 8's fresh 35. Since a single session's highest die can never exceed 8 (the biggest die is a d8), and every threshold past level 2 already exceeds 8, no single session can ever clear more than one level-up — the reset itself prevents skipping ahead. Max level 8.

Themed Question Packages

XP is awarded via end-of-session questions, each scaled by a die (d4/d6/d8) by scope — highest result is XP for everyone. The five confirmed questions work fine as a generic default, but XP questions are a campaign's actual incentive structure — they tell players what their characters should be doing. Pick a package matching the table's real focus, or mix five questions from different ones. Same mechanic throughout, only the categories change.

Leveling Procedure, Worked Example

After every session, add the highest die result to the party's current-level XP tally. If it now meets or exceeds 5× current level, everyone levels up and the tally resets to 0 — the next threshold is calculated fresh from the new, higher level.

Worked Example — Tracking XP Across Sessions

Dungeon Delvers package, a level-1 party starting at 0 XP toward a level-2 threshold of 5:

Session 1: a modest hoard (treasure, moderate — d6, result 4); a trap dodged cleverly (minor — d4, result 2). Highest: 4. Tally: 0→4. Doesn't clear 5 — stays level 1.

Session 2: someone survives Death's Door (major — d8, result 3). Tally: 4→7. Clears 5 — levels up to 2, tally resets to 0, next threshold is 5×2=10.

Session 3: the dungeon's goal is completed (major — d8, result 6). Tally: 0→6. Doesn't clear 10 — stays level 2.

Session 4: another trap solved (minor — d4, result 3). Tally: 6→9. Still doesn't clear 10 — stays level 2.

Session 5: a decent haul (moderate — d6, result 5). Tally: 9→14. Clears 10 — levels up to 3, tally resets to 0, next threshold is 5×3=15.

The reset is doing real work: even a strong single roll can never clear a threshold alone once past level 2, since the biggest possible session result (8) is always less than what's needed — every level past 2 genuinely requires multiple sessions, and no lucky roll ever skips one outright.

Vertical & Lateral Growth

Each level-up: choose Vertical (+1 to one stat — hard capped at +3, no stat can ever exceed +3 under any circumstance. A maxed 3d6 roll alone caps at +2; stacking a same-stat Background bonus on top of a maxed roll can already reach +3 at character creation, before any Vertical growth at all. A stat already at +3 simply isn't a legal target for a further pick — the player picks a different stat instead, same as any other level-up — plus +1 flat Guard, identical for everyone, uncapped) and Lateral (archetype-specific — see Archetypes for the full per-level tables). Nothing is pre-selected; every choice is made fresh at the moment it's earned.
