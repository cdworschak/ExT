A Hack of The Electrum Archive

Eberron × The Electrum Archive

Race and culture fused into a single character-creation slot. D&D classes reskinned onto three archetypes — Agent, Wayfarer, Syberist (the latter with four traditions, including the crafting-flavored Artificer). Dragonshard dust powers casting the way the underlying rules describe Elder ink working. This is the clean, play-ready ruleset: no design rationale, no test data, no history. Just what you need at the table.
