03 — Backgrounds

Backgrounds

Race and culture fused into a single choice. Every Background grants one stat bonus (or two, for a genuine racial/body-plan trait), two Talents, and gear that's narrative only — never a weapon, armor, or mount with implied stats. Your actual weapon and armor come from the separate Weapon & Armor choice in Character Creation.

Talents & Expertise

Talents grant Advantage on relevant checks — the universal expertise system every archetype has access to, tied to Background rather than class. Every Background below grants two. A GM may rule a check succeeds automatically if a character is also carrying relevant equipment (the Healing Talent plus a Healing Toolkit to treat a Wound).

Languages

Common is free to everyone. INT bonus ≤ −2 means illiterate — speaks known languages but can't read or write any. INT bonus > 0 grants +1 extra language per point. Non-human Backgrounds grant their ancestral language free, separate from the INT-based count — noted per Background below where it applies.

Dragonmarked Houses

Two distinct kinds of House-lineage Background exist for each House: Agent (works for the House, no Mark) and Marked (carries the House's Least Mark). Most people connected to a House, even by blood, never manifest a mark — Agent Backgrounds are the common case. Only a Background specifically named "[House] Marked" grants one; a career-tied Agent Background never does. Free — no shard cost, narrow and reliable.

Being Marked carries real House Duty, true for every Marked Background: the House can call on you for tasks you can't easily refuse, and Excoriation — being cast out, marked but disowned — is a genuine, heavy consequence for defying them seriously, not a minor setback. Less personal freedom, offset by real power.

Aberrant Mark — open to any Background, mark-touched by accident rather than House-blooded. Grants the ability to attempt a Minor-tier cast (Attack or Effect) with zero shards ever possessed — every attempt is a genuine Overcast, resolved entirely through the existing Overcast Backlash Pool (always reduced to 3d6, since 0 shards is always more than half short of any possible Minor cost). No new mechanic — reuses casting and Overcasting directly. Own Noun category for Spell Names: Aberrant.

Mark Tier Power Baseline

Least ≈ Spark tier (small, real, narrow), Lesser ≈ Minor tier (a real step up, reliable utility), Greater ≈ Major tier (rare, breaks the laws of nature), Siberys ≈ Mythic tier (unique, world-altering, essentially GM-placed only). Not an exact mechanical translation — a power-level anchor for what each tier should feel like when building one out.

Shifters, Kalashtar, Changelings, Warforged

Shifters

Every Shifter shares one race-wide trait, true for all three Backgrounds below: once per encounter in combat, call on their heritage briefly — Advantage on a single melee attack as claws or fangs extend, or move one extra zone this Round as a speed burst. Once per scene in a social situation, letting their heritage show through the same way grants Advantage on an intimidation-based check. Shifters carry real prejudice in most of Khorvaire — their lycanthropic heritage is visible proof of what they are, and using either version of this trait openly makes that harder to hide, not easier.

Dragonmarked Heir (template)

Distinct from a career-tied House Background — most people in a marked family never manifest a Dragonmark. This is specifically someone the mark chose. Worked example, Lyrandar Heir — +1 CHA (swap for the House's thematic stat elsewhere). Talents: Marked (carries the House's Least Mark for free, scales with level), Named Heir (real standing within the House hierarchy — Advantage on checks invoking it). Gear: a signet marking blood, a sealed letter of provisional inheritance, a small elemental-bound trinket.

Excoriate Heir — the same template with the trade-off made concrete. Marked stays unchanged. Named Heir flips to Disadvantage with anyone loyal to the House — a disowned traitor, not blood. Available as a starting Background in its own right, not just a mid-campaign consequence.

Building Your Own Background

The 64 Backgrounds above follow one pattern, if you want to build another.

Worked Example — Sharn Cutpurse

Name: Sharn + Cutpurse.

Stat bonus: +1 DEX.

Talent 1 (combat/adventuring): Quick Fingers — Advantage on fast, precise hand-work under time pressure: lockpicking, disarming, a quick draw.

Talent 2 (flavor): Knows a Guy — one fence or fixer contact.

Gear: lockpicks, a stashed bolt-hole key.

Race-wide trait: none.
