03 — Backgrounds

Backgrounds

Race and culture fused into a single choice. Every Background grants one stat bonus (or two, for a genuine racial/body-plan trait), two Talents, and gear that's narrative only — never a weapon, armor, or mount with implied stats. Your actual weapon and armor come from the separate Weapon & Armor choice in Character Creation.

Talents & Expertise

Talents grant Advantage on relevant checks — the universal expertise system every archetype has access to, tied to Background rather than class. Every Background below grants two. A GM may rule a check succeeds automatically if a character is also carrying relevant equipment (the Healing Talent plus a Healing Toolkit to treat a Wound).

Languages

Common is free to everyone. INT bonus ≤ −2 means illiterate — speaks known languages but can't read or write any. INT bonus > 0 grants +1 extra language per point. Non-human Backgrounds grant their ancestral language free, separate from the INT-based count — noted per Background below where it applies.

Half-Elves (Khoravar)

Khoravar are a genuine third culture, not a hybrid category — most are generations removed from any elven ancestor they could name, with their own creole (Khoravar Cant) and institutional weight through two predominantly Khoravar-blooded Houses (Lyrandar, Medani). Both elven traditions explicitly reject them.

Dragonmarked Heir (template)

Distinct from a career-tied House Background — most people in a marked family never manifest a Dragonmark. This is specifically someone the mark chose. Worked example, Lyrandar Heir — +1 CHA (swap for the House's thematic stat elsewhere). Talents: Marked (carries the House's Least Mark for free, scales with level), Named Heir (real standing within the House hierarchy — Advantage on checks invoking it). Gear: a signet marking blood, a sealed letter of provisional inheritance, a small elemental-bound trinket.

Excoriate Heir — the same template with the trade-off made concrete. Marked stays unchanged. Named Heir flips to Disadvantage with anyone loyal to the House — a disowned traitor, not blood. Available as a starting Background in its own right, not just a mid-campaign consequence.
