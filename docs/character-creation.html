02 — Character Creation

Character Creation

The full character creation sequence, in one continuous flow.

The Full Sequence

Stat Bonus Generation

Roll 3d6 per stat (summed), look up the bonus. Changed from 2d6 specifically to make the extremes rarer and narrow the total spread — 3d6 keeps a genuine bell curve with more room in the tails to tune how rare ±2 actually is.

No stat can ever exceed +3, under any circumstance. A maxed 3d6 roll alone caps at +2; stacking a same-stat Background bonus on a maxed roll can already reach +3 at creation. Vertical growth (see Advancement) can also reach +3 for a stat that didn't max out this way — but once any stat hits +3, it's simply not a legal target for further Vertical picks; the player points those at a different stat instead.

Mercy adjustment: if no stat is positive after rolling, raise two stats to +1 each, or one stat to +2 (player's choice). Applies to the raw roll, before Background bonuses stack.

Choice Guidance

What makes each step's decision strong or weak — tested against real builds, not abstract advice.

Background

Pick one whose stat bonus lands on the stat your archetype actually wants. A mismatched Background isn't illegal, just a real, felt inefficiency unless the concept specifically calls for it.

Archetype

Wayfarer = frontline durability, Agent = breadth and narrative leverage, Syberist = real spellcasting (including Artificer, the crafting-flavored tradition). If torn, ask which resource (Grit / Leverage / shards) sounds more fun to spend during play. If Wayfarer or Agent, decide Wandslinger here too — it sets a cast stat immediately, which matters for Guard Stat later.

Rolling & the Mercy Rule

If mercy fires, raising one stat to +2 concentrates power; raising two to +1 spreads it. The mercy check applies to the raw roll, before any Background bonus stacks on top.

Weapon & Armor

Decide melee-or-ranged first, then which stat governs it — DEX doesn't automatically mean ranged. Check the relevant stat against Weapon Proficiency before committing narratively to a concept; if it fails, fall back to the highest tier you qualify for, or go ranged instead. Worth remembering here: whatever you pick directly decides your Guard Stat pool, calculated next.

Guard Stat

No longer a choice — it's automatically your highest-bonus stat, excluding your cast stat and whatever your equipped weapon(s) use. A Heavy weapon on your best physical stat locks that stat out of Guard, so a genuinely tanky build often wants its offensive stat and its naturally-high stat to be different from the start.

Languages

A high-INT build should pick languages that actually matter for the campaign. A −2-or-lower INT build's illiteracy is a real constraint worth embracing, not working around — it's supposed to bite.

Starting Wealth

A caster should convert a meaningful chunk of the 200gp into refined shards — 100–150gp is reasonable. A pure martial build can spend close to zero on shards and put everything into gear instead.

Safety Valve If a player is unhappy with the whole character — not a single bad roll the mercy rule already covers, but the build overall not feeling right — player and GM can agree to start over completely. Always available by mutual agreement, no restrictions.
Worked Example — Building Tavin

1. Background. The player wants a healer with real standing in the world. Backgrounds lists Jorasco Marked — +1 WIS, the Mark of Healing, and House Duty. Locked in.

2. Archetype. Real spellcasting fits the concept best. Archetypes → Syberist, Flamesworn tradition (CHA cast stat, Silver Flame flavor).

3. Roll stats. 3d6 ×6, six independent rolls: 14, 11, 10, 9, 8, 13. Looked up: +1, 0, 0, −1, −1, +1. At least one is positive, so no Mercy needed.

4. Assign freely. These six bonuses can go to any stat, in any order. CHA is Tavin's cast stat, so the highest roll (+1) goes there. DEX gets the other +1, since it governs the weapon. The two 0s go to WIS and INT; the two −1s land on CON and STR — Tavin isn't built to take hits or force doors open, and that's a deliberate trade, not an oversight.

5. Apply Background. Jorasco Marked's +1 WIS lands on top of the just-assigned WIS 0, making it +1.

6–7. Weapon & Armor (see Inventory & Items). DEX +1 qualifies for Medium melee or ranged. Tavin takes a Medium weapon (a warded rod, DEX) and Light armor — nothing that would slow down a support caster.

8. Guard Stat, automatic. Excludes CHA (cast stat) and DEX (weapon stat). Of what's left — WIS +1, INT 0, CON −1 — WIS is highest, so Guard Stat lands there on its own.

9–10. Guard & Wounds (see Combat). Guard = 4 + WIS(+1) + Light armor(+1) = 6. One Wound slot, as every character starts with.

11. Starting wealth. 200gp. Tavin puts 120gp into refined shards (12 shards) for a real casting reserve, and the rest into a Healing Toolkit and basics.

Tavin is now a level-1 Flamesworn Syberist: CHA +1 (cast), WIS +1 (Guard Stat), Guard 6, one Wound slot, a warded rod, Light armor, 12 refined shards, and the Least Mark of Healing free from Background — ready to play.
