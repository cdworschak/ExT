03 — Combat

Combat

Resolution, weapons, maneuvers, magic items, the economy, wounds, and everything that governs a fight from surprise to the last swing.

Combat Procedure

Core Resolution

Roll 2d10 + stat bonus, succeed on 12 or higher. Advantage/Disadvantage: roll 3d10, keep the best or worst two.

Mercy adjustment: if no stat is positive after rolling, raise two stats to +1 each, or one stat to +2 (player's choice). Applies to the raw roll, before Background bonuses stack.

Item Slots

Ten item slots total: 2 hand, 2 body, 6 backpack. Hand slots hold what's actively wielded; body slots hold worn gear for easy access in combat; backpack slots hold everything else. Retrieving a backpack item during combat costs an action.

  • Shards and coin are abstracted resources, not slot items — tracked as numbers, never occupying a slot regardless of quantity.
  • A slot holds one distinct item, or a reasonable stack of a single consumable type (a quiver of arrows, several days of rations, a handful of torches) — not weight-tracked or itemized down to the individual arrow.
  • At capacity, picking up something new means dropping or stowing something else first.
  • Shield/Tower Shield sit in a hand slot, same category as a weapon.

Melee and ranged proficiency are symmetric — both gate on the same thresholds at each tier, just checking STR for melee and DEX for ranged.

Heavy weapons are always Two-handed. All ranged weapons are Two-handed too, at any tier — reloading needs both hands regardless of size. Only Light/Medium melee can pair with a Shield.

Melee vs. ranged is a separate axis from which stat governs the weapon. A DEX-based finesse weapon (rapier, dagger) is still melee — Two-handed only at Heavy, Shield-compatible otherwise. Only genuinely ranged weapons are always Two-handed, regardless of which stat governs them.

Casting stat and weapon stat are always separate. A caster's cast stat is never used for weapon damage, even if it's their defining stat — a weapon they carry uses STR or DEX like anyone else's.

Critical hit: doubles on the to-hit roll (both counted dice match) on a successful hit = maximum weapon damage, no damage roll.

Magic Items

Charged/consumables priced at the tier's casting cost. Permanent items priced at 10–20× the tier's average casting cost — Cantrip/Spark in raw shards, Minor and above in refined shards.

Economy

Raw shards: 5cp each (= 1 arrow), power Cantrip/Spark. Refined shards: 10gp each, power Minor+ and permanent magic items. Starting wealth: 200gp, flexible — spend on refined shards, gear, or both.

Guard & Wounds

Guard = 4 + Guard Stat bonus + archetype bonus + armor + shield, floor 1. Restores fully on a short rest. 1 Wound slot at creation, second at level 4, third at level 8.

An open Wound reduces max Guard by 2; current Guard refills to the new max, floor 1. The next roll is made with Disadvantage ("Reeling"), plus a Wound Complication (d6). Wounds don't heal on a short rest and can't close mid-combat.

At 0 Guard with all Wound slots full: roll d6 — 1–3 dies, 4+ faints and rolls on the Scar table.

Healing Magic

Combat Mending (in combat): restores Guard to current max, can't close a Wound. Field Dressing (out of combat, a full Exploration Turn): closes one Wound.

Zones & Range

Three zones: Melee, Near, Far. Move (0–1 zone) and Act are both available the same Round. Melee weapons hit same zone only; ranged/thrown hit same zone + 1 adjacent.

Disengaging: leaving a zone with a live melee hostile requires a DEX check (2d10+DEX vs 12) or they get a free attack first.

Initiative

2d10 + DEX + weapon modifier − armor penalty, once per fight, fixed order.

Surprise & Ambushes

Each character rolls 2d10+WIS individually. First success can warn others (Advantage for anyone yet to roll). Failure means skipping the Round 1 action only.

Morale

Base Morale 7 (2d6 roll-under-or-equal to pass), +2 while a named leader is alive. Checks fire once each at: first casualty, 50% losses, leader down.
